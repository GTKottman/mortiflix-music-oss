# Rondo

Home, away, home, further away, home. The theme is a friend the listener learns to wait for; each detour makes its
return feel warmer.

## How it moves

- Mark every section's **"material"**: `A` for the theme, `B`, `C`… for the detours. The first section is `A`; `A`
  comes back at least three times, never twice in a row, between at least two different detours.
- **Returns are recognisable**: each `A` keeps half or more of the first `A`'s notes. Change the accompaniment, the
  register of a counter-line, the drums, not the theme itself.
- **Detours go somewhere**: a new key area (the theme transposed, briefly), a call and response between two parts,
  a different groove. Make each detour unlike the last.

## Traps

- Detours that are just the theme again with more drums: they need their own material.
- A last return that's identical to the first: change what's under it.
