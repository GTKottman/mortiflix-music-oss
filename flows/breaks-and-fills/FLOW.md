# Breaks and fills

A kit for arranging a whole track: the main loops, plus the short loops that go between them. Fills lead into a new
section, breaks clear the floor, stop-time hits make the listener lean in.

## How the loops relate

- **Main loops** (4 or 8 bars) and **joiners** (1 or 2 bars): ask for "Mixed" lengths in the brief, or plan them.
- **A fill**: a loop (or a loop's last bar) with 1.5× the drum hits of the bars before, bringing in toms or new sounds.
- **A break**: a bar where one part is left alone, next to a full loop.
- **Stop-time**: bars where every part hits together, then silence.

## Traps

- Fills that are just more hats: bring in toms, a new rhythm, a quote of the hook.
