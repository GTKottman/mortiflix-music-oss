# Call and answer

Each loop is a short conversation: one part plays a phrase and rests, another answers (3+ exchanges across the kit).
Different loops answer the same call in different ways, so they can follow each other.

## How the loops relate

- **Two voices** on their own channels in the loops where they talk.
- **The call stays recognisable**; the answers change.

## Traps

- Both voices playing all the time: the rests make the conversation.
