# A/B pairs

Verse and chorus energy, as loops. Loops come in pairs (1-2, 3-4…): the first of each pair is the calm twin, the
second the busy twin of the same idea. Switching between them is an arrangement.

## How the loops relate

- **An even number of loops**, the odd one of each pair at least 2 calmer (asked) and measurably calmer.
- **Twins share material**: the busy twin keeps 40-80% of the calm one and changes the rest (a variation).
- Different pairs are different ideas.

## Traps

- Twins that are the same loop with more hats: change the rhythm or harmony too.
