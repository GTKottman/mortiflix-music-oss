# Drop

Tension, a held breath, release. The build makes the listener lean in; the gap makes them wait; the drop pays it off
with everything at once.

## How it moves

- **Introduce the riff early**, incomplete. The listener needs to know what's coming.
- **The build** rises bar by bar (density, velocity, register): a real buildup, measured within its section.
- **The gap** is its own section of 1-2 bars, right before the drop, quiet (intensity 3 or less): almost nothing,
  one sound of the riff left hanging.
- **The drop** lands with 2+ more parts than the bar before it, 1.5× busier: everything the build promised.
- **After the drop**, keep the energy for a while, then strip back.

## Traps

- A drop that isn't bigger than the build's last bar: the gap makes it feel big, so the gap has to be real.
- Tempo tricks: the grid holds in this flow (no rubato, ritardando or accelerando).
