# Ostinato trance

The pattern is the floor and the clock. It starts in the first bar and plays to the last, unchanged. Everything else
(harmony under it, parts against it, the swell of the whole) changes around it, so the listener hears the same notes
differently as the piece goes on.

## How it moves

- **One ostinato, untouched**, on one channel, from start to end (a 1-bar pattern repeated, or a 2-bar one).
- **Something against it**: a cross-rhythm (attacks every ¾ or 1½ beats) that rubs against the pattern's grid.
- **Swells, not drops**: energy arrives and recedes in slow swells. Nothing stops the pattern: no stop-time, no
  breaks, no drops.
- **Harmony moves under the ostinato** so its notes change colour.

## Traps

- Changing the ostinato "for variety": the point is that it doesn't change.
- Too much going on: one or two things move at a time.
