# Through-composed

A journey, not a song. No section comes back; each grows out of the one before. Unity comes from development: a small
cell keeps changing shape, so new music still sounds related.

## How it moves

- **No repeats**: no section replays another (60%+), no ostinato, no riff.
- **A cell that develops**: transposed, sequenced (a figure moved by the same step 3+ times), fragmented, slowed: 3+
  different transformations of the motif (development).
- **Transitions grow out of the material**: the end of each section plants the start of the next.

## Traps

- A collage: unrelated sections in a row. Each one must grow from the last.
