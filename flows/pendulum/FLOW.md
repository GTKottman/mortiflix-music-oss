# Pendulum

Back and forth, further each time. The listener learns the rhythm of the swings and braces for each one; the widening
is what keeps it from feeling like repetition.

## How it moves

- **Alternate**: every section moves the other way from the one before (calm, storm, calm, storm…), 4+ sections.
- **Widen**: each swing is at least as wide as the one before. Plan the intensities so the differences grow:
  4 → 6 → 3 → 8 → 2 → 9.
- **Inside the melody too**: a pendulum figure (notes swinging between two registers) belongs in a lead or bass line.
- Crescendos take you into the storms; diminuendos bring you out.

## Traps

- Swings that stay the same size: that's a loop, not a pendulum.
- A drone or pedal underneath everything: it pins the pendulum still (this flow avoids them).
