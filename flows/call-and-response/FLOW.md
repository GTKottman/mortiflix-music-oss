# Call and response

A conversation. One part plays a phrase and stops; another answers. As the piece goes on the gaps shrink, the phrases
overlap, and at the peak the two voices interlock note by note (a hocket).

## How it moves

- **Two voices** on their own channels: different registers or rhythms so the listener can tell them apart.
- **Call and response**: one plays while the other rests, then they swap (3+ exchanges).
- **Question and answer** inside a phrase too: an open ending (not on the tonic), answered by one that lands home.
- **The hocket** at the peak: the two voices interlock on alternating 16ths, never on the same one.

## Traps

- Both voices playing all the time: the rests are what make it a conversation.
