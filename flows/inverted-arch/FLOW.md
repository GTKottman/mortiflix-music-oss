# Inverted arch

The aftermath. It opens at full strength (the peak is in the first 30%) and the rest of the piece is that energy
draining away. Melancholy without a slow intro: the loss is the shape.

## How it moves

- **Open big**: the most intense section starts in the first 30%.
- **Come apart**: the motif fragments (pieces of it played on their own), parts drop out, velocity falls.
- **Slow down without changing the tempo**: the motif augmented (its rhythm 1.5× or more longer) in the late sections.
- **End quiet**: the last section at 3 or less, well under the peak.

## Traps

- A slow intro "to set the scene": this flow starts at the peak.
- Building again in the middle: no buildups, no drops.
