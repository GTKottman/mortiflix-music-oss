# Staircase

A climb you can count. Each section is a step: the same idea, one notch more. The listener always knows where they
are and can feel the next step coming, which makes the top feel inevitable.

## How it moves

- **Equal steps.** Sections of similar length; each adds exactly one thing (a part, a doubling, a register, a denser
  rhythm). Name what each step adds in its function.
- **The melody climbs too.** An ascending staircase figure (a short figure repeated, a step higher each time) belongs
  in the hook or a counter-line, so the steps are heard inside the phrases as well as between sections.
- **The motif jumps an octave** on the last step or two (octave displacement): the top of the stairs.
- **No step back.** Every section measures at least as busy as the one before.

## Traps

- Steps that are too small to hear: if a step only adds a shaker, it isn't a step.
- Stepping by drums only. Climb in register and harmony as well.
