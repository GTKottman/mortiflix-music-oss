# Energy ladder

A kit you can arrange by dragging the loops in order. Loop 1 is the bare groove; every loop after it keeps what came
before and adds one layer, so the kit climbs from intro energy to the drop.

## How the loops relate

- **Rising**: every loop measures at least as busy as the one before.
- **One riff** runs through the kit: hinted, then whole, then doubled. Name each loop's addition in its function.
- **Same length, same key**: they have to follow each other seamlessly.

## Traps

- A loop that swaps parts instead of adding: the ladder is cumulative.
