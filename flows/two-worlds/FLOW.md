# Two worlds

Two characters, kept apart, then brought together. The first half lets the listener get to know each one; the meeting
is the payoff.

## How it moves

- Mark every section's **"material"**: `A` for the first world, `B` for the second, `A+B` where they meet. Each world
  gets at least one section of its own before they meet.
- **Make them unlike each other**: different registers, rhythms, harmonic colour (one major-ish, one modal), different
  parts on different channels.
- **They talk before they meet**: a call and response between one part from each world.
- **When they meet**, both are heard at once (the check wants 20%+ of each world's notes in the `A+B` section), moving in
  contrary motion: one rises while the other falls.

## Traps

- Worlds that are too similar: the meeting means nothing.
- A meeting that's just one world louder.
