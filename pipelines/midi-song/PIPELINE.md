# Song

Makes a whole song as MIDI for its owner, one stage at a time.

The owner walks nine stages in order and OKs each one at its gate: Idea, Genre, Song map, Chords, Shapes, Melody,
Arrange, Feel, Export. Nothing of a stage is made before the stage before it passed. The end product is MIDI files
(the whole song, one per part, one per section) and the steps to import them into the DAW.

v1 (2026-10-23): the stages and gates only. Each stage's own work comes one version at a time
(Design Docs/MIDI Studio - Plan v1.md).
